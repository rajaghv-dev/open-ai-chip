## Act 2: Global routing (grt): demand against capacity per tile; hot columns are the power stripes.

Look for: no tile over 100 percent: the design routed clean.

Here is the routing congestion view for the kv_attn_n8 design:

![kv_attn_n8 Routing congestion (grt)](http://127.0.0.1:8770/img/engine_kv_attn_n8_congestion.png)

The color in the image represents the demand for routing tracks versus the available capacity per grid tile. The vertical columns with high color intensity are power stripes using the metal tracks. A value of 100% or more would indicate overflow (GRT-0116).

According to the metrics, the global routing wirelength is 54889, with 8443 vias. There were no DRC errors in the routing layer.
